RETREATUI v1.0.8
=================

COMBAT SAFETY AND UI LAYER FIX
- Prevented Bloodmage spell-row rebuilds during combat lockdown.
- Spellbook and Eternal Curse updates are deferred until combat ends.
- Target-debuff bars are created and anchored outside combat only.
- RetreatUI class icons, trackers and the power bar now disappear while the World Map is open.
- Animated Blood and Lunge tracking from v1.0.7 remain included.
- RetreatUI and RetreatUI_Classes both report version 1.0.8.
